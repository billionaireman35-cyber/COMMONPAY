# Regulatory and Compliance

Nigeria-first operating strategy.

Before real-money operations, obtain qualified Nigerian legal and regulatory
advice on the precise activities, fund flows and licences required.

Assess:
- CBN payment service permissions.
- Wallet and stored-value requirements.
- Customer fund safeguarding.
- AML/CFT and customer due diligence.
- Sanctions screening.
- Transaction monitoring.
- Consumer protection and complaints.
- Data protection obligations.
- Operational resilience and reporting.
- Applicable SEC requirements for virtual-asset activities.

Flutterwave and Paystack permissions do not automatically authorise
CommonPay to conduct regulated activities.

Customer wallets and independently maintained balances are a long-term
CommonPay objective, subject to its own required regulatory authorisation.
