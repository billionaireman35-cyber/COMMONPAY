# CommonPay Product Specification

## Identity
CommonPay is an AI-powered Financial Command Centre by Goldx Technologies.

Positioning: Your money. Your control. One command centre.

## Product environments
- Personal
- Business

## Product pillars
- Financial overview
- Accounts and balances
- Collections and payments
- Transfers and disbursements
- FX and exchange
- Treasury and liquidity
- Scheduled payment workflows
- Financial reporting
- Developer infrastructure
- AI financial intelligence
- Security and compliance

## Strategic direction
CommonPay begins with approved payment infrastructure partners and is
architected to eventually operate its own appropriately licensed customer
wallet and account infrastructure.

## Product principles
- Transparent fees and exchange rates.
- No fabricated balances or transaction history.
- No hidden payment deductions.
- Clear transaction status and audit history.
- Customer consent for financial actions.
- AI assists; deterministic financial services control execution.
