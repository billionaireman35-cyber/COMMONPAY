# Development Roadmap

## Foundation
- Product and domain architecture.
- Provider-independent payment interfaces.
- Financial domain and ledger invariants.
- Developer API boundary.
- AI safety boundary.
- Personal and Business frontend shell.

## Next engineering requirements
- Durable database models and migrations.
- Transactional ledger persistence.
- Merchant identity and API key lifecycle.
- Payment verification endpoints.
- Durable webhook event processing.
- Reconciliation workers.
- FX quotes and treasury services.
- KYC and compliance provider integrations.
- MFA and full authorisation.
- AI provider adapter and authorised tools.
- Observability, alerts and recovery.

## Production gates
No live customer money movement until financial controls, provider contracts,
regulatory permissions, security reviews and operational readiness are verified.
